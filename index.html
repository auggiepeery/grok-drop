Research

Four scales of the same question.

Whether it's aerosol droplets or planetesimals, aggregation follows surprisingly universal rules. We study them across scales.

Coalescence Dynamics

When two bodies touch, what survives? High-resolution models of contact, merging and mass conservation.

Molecular Aggregation

About

An open lab for the physics of growth.

Singularity Labs is a fictional, independent research collective. We build simulations that make aggregation intuitive, from rigorous large-scale runs to small interactive models you can play with in a browser.

  • Open by default. Code, data and preprints are public.
  • Interactive first. If you can't touch it, you can't feel it.
  • Scale-free thinking. Same rules, from droplets to stars.
